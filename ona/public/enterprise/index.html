Ona for enterprise

Your AI software engineer for enterprise

Runs entirely in your VPC

All Ona development environments and Ona AI agents operate entirely within your cloud infrastructure. Source code never leaves your perimeter, and there's no exposure to public internet or third-party services.

Secure onboarding

Developers use secure, temporary, fine-grained access by default, eliminating the need for manual cloud console logins and removing delays from IT approvals.

Automate environment setup

Developers spend weeks configuring development environments. A single click launches a fully prepared environment, eliminating the need to manually install tools and dependencies, or configure cloud and database access.

Delegate to background agents

Explore, scope and delegate your tasks to your personal team of professional software engineering agents. Always know what's ready for review, what's in progress, and what's been modified. Keep momentum on any device, starting a task from your phone and taking over on your iPad or laptop.

Work in parallel

Give multiple tasks to an agent in parallel. If you want to edit code manually before it's pushed for review, use VS Code embedded directly in your environment, or open an editor on your desktop.

Work on any device

Adapt work around your life. Research features on your way to work or fire off a task when you leave the office. Pick up where you left off without ever losing context of what's happening from your laptop.

Use your favorite tools

Ona can pull in context from your favorite tools: source control, issue trackers, coding assistants, editors, cloud providers and databases.

Enterprise identity and access management

Seamless integration with your existing identity providers enables centralized management of developer and agent permissions. Enforce RBAC consistently across all development activities.
